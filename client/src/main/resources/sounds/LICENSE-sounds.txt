Modifications (tools/sounds/process_sounds.py), all clips 16-bit 44.1 kHz mono WAV:
  Junkala clips: a 5 ms linear fade-out at the end, then peak-normalized to -6 dBFS.
  thrust_loop.wav: the OGG decoded with ffmpeg to 44.1 kHz mono; 2.0 s cut starting at 0.3 s; the
    first 0.25 s crossfaded (equal-power sin/cos) with the 0.25 s following the cut, so it loops
    without a click; normalized to -24 dBFS RMS.
