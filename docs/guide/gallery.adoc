Everything below was drawn by the JXPilot client's own drawing code, which follows the XPilot client's
look (<<08-building.adoc#gallery-pictures,how the pictures are made>>). Most pictures are enlarged
twice; the animated ones loop at the game's 14 frames per second. "Map symbol" is the character that
puts the feature into a map file (<<06-config-and-maps.adoc#map-symbols,Map symbols>>).

[[on-screen-map]]
== Map features

[cols="4,1,5",options="header"]
|===
|Picture |Map symbol |What it is

|image:gallery/walls-outline.png[Walls as outlines,width=140]
image:gallery/walls-filled.png[Walls filled,width=140]
|`x` `s` `a` `w` `q`
|*Walls*, drawn as outlines (XPilot's default) or filled (the viewer's kbd:[F] key, `--filled`). Touch a
wall gently and at a shallow angle and you bounce off; hit it too hard, or unshielded too fast, and you
crash (the `...WallBounce...` and `maxUnshielded...` options,
<<05-server-options.adoc#opts-walls,Walls, crashes and bouncing>>).

|image:gallery/wall-x.png[x,width=63] image:gallery/wall-s.png[s,width=63]
image:gallery/wall-a.png[a,width=63] image:gallery/wall-w.png[w,width=63]
image:gallery/wall-q.png[q,width=63]
|`x` `s` `a` `w` `q`
|The five wall blocks: `x` full, `s` upper-left half, `a` upper-right half, `w` lower-left half,
`q` lower-right half (left to right in the pictures).

|image:gallery/decor.png[Decoration,width=140]
|`b` `h` `g` `y` `t`
|*Decoration*: drawn like walls in orange, but you fly straight through it. `b` is full, `h` `g` `y` `t`
are the four halves, like `s` `a` `w` `q`.

|image:gallery/fuel-full.png[Full fuel station,width=63]
image:gallery/fuel-half.png[Half-empty fuel station,width=63]
image:gallery/fuel-empty.png[Empty fuel station,width=63]
|`#`
|*Fuel station*: full, half empty, empty. Hold the refuel key (kbd:[F] or kbd:[Ctrl]) near it to take
fuel; the red bar shows what is left, and it refills slowly.

|image:gallery/base-up.png[Base facing up,width=77]
image:gallery/base-down.png[Base facing down,width=77]
image:gallery/base-right.png[Base facing right,width=77]
image:gallery/base-left.png[Base facing left,width=77]
|`_` `0`-`9` (`$` sets the facing)
|*Home base*: where you start and restart. The white line is the landing pad; your ship starts on it,
facing away from it. Bases face up unless gravity or an adjacent `$` says otherwise. In team play the
digit is the team that owns it.

|image:gallery/cannon-up.png[Cannon facing up,width=77]
image:gallery/cannon-down.png[Cannon facing down,width=77]
image:gallery/cannon-left.png[Cannon facing left,width=77]
image:gallery/cannon-right.png[Cannon facing right,width=77]
|`r` `c` `d` `f`
|*Cannons* (up, down, left, right), usually set into a wall. They fire at players in front of them;
shoot them to destroy them for a while (`cannonDeadTime`).

|image:gallery/cannon-dead.png[Destroyed cannon,width=77]
|
|A *destroyed cannon* disappears; on a block that carries a background dot, the dot shows instead.

|image:gallery/target.png[Target,width=63] image:gallery/target-damaged.png[Damaged target,width=63]
image:gallery/target-badly-damaged.png[Badly damaged target,width=63]
image:gallery/target-own-team.png[Own team's target,width=63]
|`!`
|*Target*: healthy, damaged, badly damaged, and one of your own team (blue). It belongs to the team with
the closest base (the digit). Shoot enemy targets to destroy them; your team repairs its own with the
repair key (kbd:[F]).

|image:gallery/treasure.png[Treasure with its ball,width=70]
image:gallery/treasure-empty.png[Empty treasure,width=70]
|`*` `^`
|*Treasure* (ball home) with its ball, and an *empty treasure*, which has no ball of its own. It belongs
to the team with the closest base. Steal the enemy's ball and bring it home to your treasure to score
(`captureTheFlag` and the ball options, <<05-server-options.adoc#opts-objectives,Treasures, balls and
targets>>).

|image:gallery/gravity-plus.png[+,width=63] image:gallery/gravity-minus.png[-,width=63]
image:gallery/gravity-clockwise.png[>,width=63] image:gallery/gravity-anticlockwise.png[<,width=63]
|`+` `-` `>` `<`
|*Gravity sources*: `+` pushes ships away, `-` pulls them in, `>` swirls them clockwise and `<`
anticlockwise.

|image:gallery/gravity-up.png[i,width=63] image:gallery/gravity-down.png[m,width=63]
image:gallery/gravity-left.png[j,width=63] image:gallery/gravity-right.png[k,width=63]
|`i` `m` `j` `k`
|*Gravity currents*: a steady pull up, down, left or right inside the block.

|image:gallery/wormhole.gif[Wormhole,width=63]
|`@` `(` `)`
|*Wormhole*: fly into it and you come out at another wormhole. `@` works both ways and `(` is an
entrance only; both look like this. `)` is an exit only and is not drawn.

|image:gallery/item-concentrator.gif[Item concentrator,width=63]
|`%`
|*Item concentrator* (three turning triangles): items appear around it
(`itemConcentratorRadius`, `itemConcentratorProb`, <<03-playing.adoc#items,Items>>).

|image:gallery/asteroid-concentrator.gif[Asteroid concentrator,width=63]
|`&`
|*Asteroid concentrator* (three turning squares): asteroids appear around it.

|image:gallery/checkpoints.png[Checkpoints,width=385]
|`A`-`Z`
|*Race checkpoints*, only in race mode (`timing`): fly through them in order, `A` first. The next one
you must reach is filled.

|
|`z`
|*Friction block*: ships inside it feel `blockFriction`. In a game, both the JXPilot and the XPilot
server show it as filled decoration (`b`) when `blockFrictionVisible` is on (the default), and not at all
otherwise. The map viewer does not draw it.

|
|space, `.`
|*Background dots*: small blue dots every 8 blocks in empty space (visible in the whole-screen picture
above), so you can see your own motion where there is nothing else.
|===

[[on-screen-objects]]
== Ships and objects

[cols="3,5",options="header"]
|===
|Picture |What it is

|image:gallery/ships.png[Your ship, a team mate and an enemy,width=300]
|*Ships*: yours (left, no name), a team mate (blue, with name) and anyone else (white, with name).
Robots look exactly like human players. While a round is about to start, ships are drawn red.

|image:gallery/ship-lights.gif[Marking lights,width=60]
|*Marking lights*: the small red and green lights blinking on a ship's sides.

|image:gallery/ship-shield.png[Shield,width=60]
image:gallery/ship-emergency-shield.png[Emergency shield,width=60]
image:gallery/ship-deflector.png[Deflector,width=60]
|*Shield* (dashed circle), *emergency shield* (a second circle) and *deflector* (blue circle).

|image:gallery/ship-cloaked.png[Cloaked ship,width=60]
image:gallery/ship-phasing.png[Phasing ship,width=60]
|A *cloaked* ship you can still see (with sensors, or a team mate) and a *phasing* ship have a dashed
hull.

|image:gallery/ship-locked.png[Locked ship,width=80]
|The player you have *locked on* (lock keys, <<03-playing.adoc#controls,Controls>>) is drawn filled.

|image:gallery/shots.png[Shots,width=100]
|*Shots*: white squares. Shots that cannot hurt you (a team mate's with team immunity, a paused player's,
your own with `selfImmunity`) are blue crosses; nuclear shots blink red.

|image:gallery/sparks-debris.gif[Sparks and debris,width=80]
|*Sparks and debris*: engine exhaust and explosion fragments, cooling from yellow to red.

|image:gallery/mines.png[Mines,width=100]
|*Mines*: someone else's mine (filled blue, left) and a team mine (outline, with its owner's name).

|image:gallery/missiles.png[Missiles,width=100]
|*Missiles*: thick white lines, pointing where they fly.

|image:gallery/laser.png[Laser,width=120]
|*Laser* beam from a ship.

|image:gallery/ecm.gif[ECM,width=100]
|*ECM* burst: a growing white circle that confuses missiles, mines and robots, and can blind players.

|image:gallery/tractor-beam.png[Tractor beam,width=140]
|*Tractor or pressor beam* between two ships: short dashes.

|image:gallery/transporter-beam.gif[Transporter beam,width=130]
|*Transporter beam*: crawling dashes while a player steals an item.

|image:gallery/refuel-beam.gif[Refuel beam,width=100]
|*Refuel beam*: crawling dashes from a refuelling ship to the fuel station. A ship repairing a target
draws the same beam to the target.

|image:gallery/ball-tether.png[Ball on its tether,width=120]
image:gallery/ball-connecting.png[Ball being picked up,width=120]
|*Ball*: carried on a solid tether (left); dashed while it is being picked up (right).

|image:gallery/paused.gif[Paused player,width=60]
|A *paused* player: a blue square with a `P`, blinking during the 10-second pause countdown
(<<03-playing.adoc#pausing,Pausing>>).

|image:gallery/wreckage.png[Wreckage,width=160]
|*Wreckage* of a destroyed ship. On an XPilot server, pieces that can still kill you are white and
harmless ones red. The JXPilot server does not mark deadly pieces; whether wreckage can kill depends on
its `wreckageCollisionMayKill` option.

|image:gallery/asteroids.png[Asteroids,width=150]
|*Asteroids* of three sizes.

|image:gallery/items-in-play.png[Items,width=125]
|*Items*: a blue triangle with the item's symbol. Every item is pictured in
<<03-playing.adoc#items,Items>>.
|===

[[on-screen-hud]]
== The HUD and the panels

[cols="3,5",options="header"]
|===
|Picture |What it is

|image:gallery/hud-box.png[The HUD,width=150]
|The *HUD* around your ship: the dashed lines, with the pieces below arranged around them.

|image:gallery/hud-lock.png[Lock indicator,width=200]
|*Lock*: the locked player's name, a small ship symbol, the distance in blocks (`012`), and the dot that
shows the direction to them.

|image:gallery/hud-items.png[Item column,width=35]
|*Items you carry*, with their counts.

|image:gallery/hud-fuel-meter.png[Fuel meter,width=25]
|*Fuel meter*: how much fuel you have.

|image:gallery/hud-time-left.png[Time left,width=70]
|*Time left* in the round or game.

|image:gallery/hud-modifiers.png[Modifiers,width=90]
|*Weapon modifiers* in use, when the server allows them (`allowModifiers`): `F` full nuclear, `N`
nuclear, `C` cluster, `I` implosion, `V` velocity, `X` mini, `Z` spread and `B` power with their levels,
and `LS`/`LB` for a stun or blinding laser. The picture shows nuclear cluster, velocity 2, spread 1.

|image:gallery/score-popup.png[Score popup,width=100]
|*Score popup*: points you just gained or lost, where it happened.

|image:gallery/fuel-gauge.png[Fuel gauge,width=250]
|*Fuel gauge* (top right).

|image:gallery/radar.png[Radar,width=268]
|*Radar* (top left): the map in miniature, your ship with its heading line, other players (team mates
blue) and objects such as asteroids.

|image:gallery/score-list.png[Score list,width=270]
|*Score list*: team, score and name; `*` marks you, `R` a robot, `D` a player out of lives, `P` a
paused player and `W` one waiting for the next round. Outside team play the first column shows
alliances: `+` for your allies, or each player's alliance number when the server announces alliances.

|image:gallery/talk-line.png[Talk line,width=420]
|*Talk line*, opened with kbd:[M].

|image:gallery/messages.png[Messages,width=320]
|*Messages*: game events and chat, fading after a while.
|===
